SC.03Director's Statement

"I never picked a lane, so I became the bridge between marketing and content."

About Davide

Most people pick a lane. I built a bridge between two. By day I think in funnels, positioning and audience psychology. By night, and often by day too, I'm behind a camera chasing the shot that stops the scroll.

Somewhere between strategy and storyboard I found the work that fits: building brands worth watching, and backing them with the numbers. Strategy gives the work a reason to exist. The camera gives it a reason to be remembered.

When it matters, I run the full chain myself: brief, shoot, edit, grade, launch, measure. Nothing gets lost between the plan and the frame.

SC.06Values
LOG.01

Story First

Every deliverable serves a story before it serves a metric. The metric follows the story, not the other way around.

LOG.02

Craft + Strategy

Beautiful without a plan is decoration. A plan without craft doesn't get watched. I keep both in the same hands.

LOG.03

Radical Authenticity

Real people, real brands, real footage. No stock gloss, no filler b-roll standing in for the truth.

LOG.04

Always Re-Cut

The first edit never ships. Neither does the first strategy. Review, cut, refine, until it earns the watch.
